## Hey there

I'm Ehsan, a full-stack developer. Most of what I build deals with **money, gold and currency**, where one wrong number means one very unhappy customer. So I like to think things through first and write code second.

Almost all of my work is private, built for clients and for the team at **Arshiv Co**. What you see here is just the tip of the iceberg.

## Things I've built

- Full crypto exchange platforms: spot, futures, staking and more
- Cross-chain bridges and blockchain scanners for different networks
- Trading bots, MetaTrader expert advisors and TradingView indicators
- Gold trading platforms for both retail customers and partner dealers
- KYC, risk scoring and deposit verification services
- Accounting and business management systems
- Telegram Mini Apps and Telegram bots
- AI agents and automation
- ...and plenty more that lives in private repos

## Outside of work

- Big fan of playing games
- Building my own games
- Modding and tinkering with existing ones, like a full Persian localization of [Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4](https://github.com/e3ang/call-of-duty-mw4-persian-farsi)
- Unity, Godot and Blender

## How I work

- Built to scale: Docker, load testing, Terraform and Ansible
- Queues, caching and monitoring for real traffic
- Automated tests and CI/CD on every project
- Clean architecture and clear docs
- Proper right-to-left Persian UIs
